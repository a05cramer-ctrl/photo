Rules

01

The line

The finish line is every hh:00:00 UTC, 24 times a day.

02

The winner

The first $PHOTO buy of at least 0.05 SOL in a block timed at or after the line. Same block, the earlier transaction wins.

03

The pot

Every creator fee from the hour before the line goes to the winner's wallet, paid within five minutes.

04

The proof

The slot, the block time and the transaction are on-chain. Anyone can check every finish on Solscan.

Why now

Solana switched to Alpenglow on Oct 21, 2026, its biggest consensus change since 2020. Blocks land every ~200 ms and are final in a fraction of a second. A race decided by the first block after the hour only works on a chain this fast.
